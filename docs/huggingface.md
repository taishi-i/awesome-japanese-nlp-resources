This page lists Japanese NLP-specific models and datasets available on Hugging Face. Currently, it includes 285 models and 170 datasets.

## Models
### text-generation
 * [japanese-gpt-neox-small](https://huggingface.co/rinna/japanese-gpt-neox-small) - 📥 505k / ⭐ 15 / A 12‑layer, 768‑hidden Japanese GPT‑NeoX model trained on CC‑100, C4, and Wikipedia, compatible with Huggingface, with an optional toy prefix‑tuning weight that forces each sentence to end with a smiling‑face emoji.
 * [llm-jp-4-33b-thinking-gguf](https://huggingface.co/llm-jp/llm-jp-4-33b-thinking-gguf) - 📥 337k / ⭐ 11 / LLM‑jp‑4‑33b‑thinking‑gguf is a 33 B Japanese language model from the National Institute of Informatics, pre‑trained with SFT and DPO (no RL), available for llama.cpp via an LLM‑jp fork, and includes detailed usage instructions in its cookbook.
 * [llm-jp-3-150m](https://huggingface.co/llm-jp/llm-jp-3-150m) - 📥 185k / ⭐ 8 / LLM‑jp‑3‑150m—a 150‑M‑parameter Japanese language model from the National Institute of Informatics’ LLM R&D Center—is distributed in Hugging Face Transformers format, requiring torch ≥ 2.3.0, transformers ≥ 4.40.1, accelerate ≥ 0.29.3, flash‑attn ≥ 2.5.8, and was pretrained on Japanese Wikipedia, Common Crawl, WARP/PDF, WARP/HTML, and Kaken data using a unigram byte‑fallback tokenizer.
 * [sarashina2.2-0.5b-instruct-v0.1](https://huggingface.co/sbintuitions/sarashina2.2-0.5b-instruct-v0.1) - 📥 131k / ⭐ 16 / SB Intuitions’ Sarashina2.2‑0.5B instruct v0.1 is a 0.5‑billion‑parameter Japanese autoregressive model that performs well on Japanese and English MT benchmarks and is ready to load via torch‑transformers.
 * [gpt-neox-japanese-2.7b](https://huggingface.co/abeja/gpt-neox-japanese-2.7b) - 📥 61k / ⭐ 59 / A 2.7‑B‑parameter Japanese GPT‑NeoX model trained on Japanese CC‑100 and OSCAR by ABEJA Inc, usable via Hugging Face Transformers pipelines or PyTorch and released under the MIT license.
 * [llm-jp-4-8b-base](https://huggingface.co/llm-jp/llm-jp-4-8b-base) - 📥 21k / ⭐ 8 / A repository that hosts the 8.6 B‑parameter llm‑jp‑4‑8b‑base transformer from the National Institute of Informatics’ LLM R&D Center, trained with pre‑training and mid‑training followed by supervised fine‑tuning and direct preference optimization (no reinforcement learning), and provides a PyTorch‑transformers usage guide.
 * [sarashina2.2-3b-instruct-v0.1](https://huggingface.co/sbintuitions/sarashina2.2-3b-instruct-v0.1) - 📥 17k / ⭐ 39 / Offers an autoregressive Japanese language model (sarashina2.2‑3B‑instruct‑v0.1) from SB Intuitions, benchmarked against other models, with example usage scripts and a note that safety training is limited.
 * [llm-jp-4-8b-thinking](https://huggingface.co/llm-jp/llm-jp-4-8b-thinking) - 📥 16k / ⭐ 52 / Provides the 8 B‑parameter LLM‑jp‑4‑8b‑thinking Japanese language model from NII, trained with pre‑/mid‑training and aligned via SFT/DPO, ready to use with torch‑transformers and detailed cookbook instructions.
 * [LFM2.5-1.2B-JP-202606](https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM2.5-1.2B-JP-202606) - 📥 10k / ⭐ 78 / LFM 2.5‑1.2B‑JP‑202606 is a high‑performance, general‑purpose Japanese chat model that outperforms comparable sub‑2 B models in knowledge, instruction following, math, code, and tool use, making it ideal for developers building culturally nuanced Japanese applications.
 * [llm-jp-3-1.8b](https://huggingface.co/llm-jp/llm-jp-3-1.8b) - 📥 9k / ⭐ 17 / A collection of Japanese large‑language models (1.8 b to 172 b beta1, with instruct variants) from NII’s R&D Center, packaged in Hugging Face Transformers format and pretrained on a mix of Japanese, English, and Web corpora totalling >1 trillion tokens, requiring torch ≥ 2.3, transformers ≥ 4.40, accelerate ≥ 0.29, and flash‑attn ≥ 2.5.
 * [Llama-3-Swallow-8B-Instruct-v0.1](https://huggingface.co/tokyotech-llm/Llama-3-Swallow-8B-Instruct-v0.1) - 📥 9k / ⭐ 21 / Llama3 Swallow is a Japanese‑enhanced Meta Llama 3 family released July 1 2024, offering 8B and 70B variants in Instruct and chat forms fine‑tuned with SFT and Chat Vector on Megatron‑LM, and benchmarked on key Japanese NLP tasks.
 * [Llama-3-70B-japanese-suzume-vector-v0.1](https://huggingface.co/mmnga/Llama-3-70B-japanese-suzume-vector-v0.1) - 📥 9k / ⭐ 4 / Experimental Japanese model created by extracting differences between lightblue/suzume‑llama‑3‑8B‑japanese and Meta‑Llama‑3‑8B‑Instruct using a chat‑vector approach, upsampled and applied to Meta‑Llama‑3‑70B‑Instruct, showing little change and planning future scaling.

 * [LFM2.5-1.2B-JP-202606-ONNX](https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM2.5-1.2B-JP-202606-ONNX) - 📥 8k / ⭐ 6 / Exports the Japanese LFM2.5‑1.2B model to ONNX for cross‑platform inference with ONNX Runtime, Transformers.js, and WebGPU, offering multiple precision variants (FP32, FP16, INT4/FP16 mix) and a recommended WebGPU‑friendly INT4+FP16 format.
